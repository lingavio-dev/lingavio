Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Lingavio collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Lingavio application.

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Lingavio is a language-learning application developed and operated by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy applies to the Lingavio Android application and related services.

By using Lingavio, you acknowledge that your information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

Lingavio does not sell users' personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you sign in using Google, Lingavio may receive information associated with your Google account, such as:

  • Your display name;
  • Your email address;
  • Your profile picture;
  • A unique account identifier.

Lingavio may also create an anonymous account identifier when you use features that do not require a registered account, such as certain online battle or matchmaking features.

Learning and profile data

Lingavio may process information related to your use of the application, including:

  • Selected native language and learning language;
  • Learning level and lesson progress;
  • Completed exercises and exam results;
  • Word repetition and spaced-repetition data;
  • Achievements, experience points, streaks, and statistics;
  • Battle results and matchmaking information;
  • Profile name and profile image selected by you;
  • Application preferences and notification settings.

Voice and microphone data

Lingavio may request access to your device microphone when you use pronunciation or voice-answer features.

Speech may be processed through Android speech-recognition services available on your device. Depending on your device and settings, audio or speech data may be transmitted to the provider of the speech-recognition service, such as Google.

Lingavio does not intentionally store raw microphone recordings on its own servers. Recognition results may be used temporarily to evaluate your pronunciation or answer.

Technical and diagnostic information

Lingavio may automatically collect technical information such as:

  • Device model and operating-system version;
  • Application version;
  • Language and regional settings;
  • Device identifiers generated by service providers;
  • Application interactions and usage events;
  • Crash reports, error logs, and performance information;
  • Approximate technical location derived from an IP address.

This information is generally used for analytics, security, troubleshooting, and improvement of the application.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts;
  • Save and synchronize learning progress;
  • Provide lessons, exercises, pronunciation tools, and battles;
  • Personalize learning content and repetition schedules;
  • Process and restore premium subscriptions;
  • Send lesson reminders and other requested notifications;
  • Maintain application security and prevent abuse;
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems;
  • Understand how features are used;
  • Improve Lingavio's content, stability, and user experience;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Google Firebase Services

Lingavio uses Google Firebase services. Depending on the features you use, Firebase may process information on behalf of Lingavio.

Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication is used to support Google sign-in and anonymous authentication. It may process account identifiers, email addresses, display names, profile images, authentication tokens, and related technical data.

Firebase Realtime Database

Firebase Realtime Database may be used to store or synchronize account information, learning progress, user profiles, matchmaking data, battle status, and related application data.

Lingavio currently uses Firebase infrastructure located in the European region where configured, including the europe-west1 region.

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics may collect application usage events and technical information to help understand how users interact with Lingavio and to improve the application.

Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, device information, application state, and technical logs to help identify and fix errors.

4. Google Play Billing

Lingavio may offer premium features through subscriptions processed by Google Play.

Payments are processed by Google. Lingavio does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Lingavio may receive information necessary to verify and manage a purchase, such as:

  • Purchased product or subscription identifier;
  • Purchase token;
  • Subscription status;
  • Transaction status and renewal information.

Your purchases are also subject to Google Play's terms and privacy practices.

5. Local Storage on Your Device

Lingavio stores certain information locally on your device using technologies such as Android DataStore and Room Database.

Locally stored data may include:

  • Application settings;
  • Learning progress and repetition data;
  • Downloaded or cached lesson information;
  • Notification preferences;
  • Temporary application data.

Local data may be removed when you clear the application's data or uninstall Lingavio, although synchronized data may remain in cloud services until it is deleted separately.

6. Permissions

Lingavio may request the following Android permissions:

  • Internet access: required for authentication, synchronization, online battles, purchases, analytics, and other online services.
  • Microphone access: used only when you activate pronunciation or voice-recognition features.
  • Notification permission: used to provide lesson reminders and other notifications you enable.
  • Receive boot completed: may be used to restore scheduled reminders after the device restarts.

You can manage application permissions through your Android device settings. Some features may not work when a required permission is denied.

7. Sharing of Information

Lingavio does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate the application, including:

  • Google Firebase;
  • Google Play services;
  • Android speech-recognition providers;
  • Cloud hosting and technical infrastructure providers;
  • Authorities when disclosure is legally required.

Service providers may process data under their own privacy terms and applicable data-protection obligations.

8. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, Lingavio may process personal data under one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide the application and features requested by you.
  • Consent: where you grant optional permissions or consent to specific processing.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure, maintain, analyze, and improve Lingavio.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is required by law.

9. Data Retention

Lingavio retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Account and synchronized learning data may be retained while your account remains active. Technical logs and analytics information may be retained according to the retention settings of the relevant service provider.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups after deletion before being automatically removed through normal backup-rotation processes.
